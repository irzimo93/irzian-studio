# IRZIAN STUDIO — Brand Charter

## Name
**IRZIAN STUDIO** is the approved umbrella brand name and spelling.

## Positioning
An independent digital studio building software, automation, and digital products with a long-term outlook.

## Promise
**Built with Trust. Made to Last.**  
**ساخته‌شده با اعتماد، ماندگار برای آینده**

## Character
- Trustworthy and calm
- Clear, useful, and responsible
- Modern without chasing short-lived trends
- Ambitious, but grounded in delivery

## Visual direction
A restrained dark foundation with violet accents, silver/soft-white typography, generous spacing, and precise geometry. The visual system should feel calm, durable, and premium rather than loud.

## Brand architecture
The umbrella brand may contain these product families:
- IRZIAN Software
- IRZIAN Labs
- IRZIAN Digital
- IRZIAN Services

These are working divisions under one brand, not claims of separate legal companies.

## Naming rule
Use **IRZIAN** consistently. Do not silently change spelling, spacing, or capitalization in official brand assets.
