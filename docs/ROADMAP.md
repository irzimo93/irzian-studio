# Product & Business Roadmap

This roadmap is staged. A later phase does not begin merely because it is listed; it begins after its prerequisites are met.

## Phase 0 — Foundation
- Establish the official name, promise, and visual direction.
- Keep the source of truth in this repository.
- Publish a lightweight informational website.
- Document ownership, access, backups, and release practices.

## Phase 1 — First useful products
- Select and publish real software tools.
- Add product pages, documentation, support, and version history.
- Keep downloads and product claims accurate.

## Phase 2 — Storefront
- Add a catalog for software, services, and digital products.
- Define product metadata, pricing, refund/support policies, and order records.
- Add customer accounts only when there is a clear need.
- Choose a payment provider only after availability, compliance, fees, and security are reviewed.

## Phase 3 — Services and automation
- Publish clearly scoped service offers.
- Add inquiry and project intake workflows.
- Establish delivery, support, and privacy procedures.

## Phase 4 — Digital collectibles / NFTs
- Begin with a curated showcase and provenance records.
- Add wallet connection, minting, or marketplace integrations only after security, legal, tax, and platform reviews.
- Never promise investment returns or imply guaranteed resale value.

## Phase 5 — Long-term stewardship
- Maintain a written history of major decisions.
- Document role transfer, account recovery, domain renewal, and repository access.
- Keep the brand and its records understandable to future maintainers and generations.
