# Governance & Continuity

IRZIAN STUDIO is intended to remain understandable and maintainable over a long time horizon.

## Operating rules
1. Keep official decisions in version-controlled documents.
2. Use named releases and meaningful commit messages.
3. Enable two-factor authentication on critical accounts.
4. Keep at least two trusted, authorized maintainers as the organization grows.
5. Never place passwords, API keys, private keys, recovery codes, or customer personal data in this repository.
6. Review domain ownership, billing, account recovery, and access permissions periodically.
7. Record who has authority to approve releases, spending, and changes to the brand.

## Succession
Future transfer of responsibility must follow applicable law and platform terms. Maintain an offline continuity record for account recovery and ownership documentation; do not publish sensitive details here.

## Change control
Changes to the brand name, promise, core visual identity, or governance principles should be documented with date, reason, and approval.
